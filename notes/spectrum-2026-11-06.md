# Spectrum in the pipe, outside the language (2026-11-06)

Decided with Roland: FFT lives in the pipe, not the language. This
answers the spectral gap in notes/graph-2026-11-02.md for now. Option B
there (whole-array functions at the steps of a stepped array) stays in
reserve, for spectral processing inside a loop, if patches ever want it.

    aither song.aither --solo horn | tools/fft 1024 \
      | AITHER_SOCK=/tmp/s.sock aither spectral.aither | tools/ifft 1024 | tools/play

## The idea

A bin is a complex number, and a complex number is a pair. So a spectrum
is a stream of pairs, and a patch that reads it is an ordinary patch,
one sample at a time. A mono signal at 50% overlap gives N / 2 bins every
N / 2 samples: one pair a sample, the signal's own rate. A frame is a row
and a bin a pixel, as on tv: `pixel_index(N / 2, 1)` is the bin, and an
array stepped `every = N / 2` steps once a frame. Nothing was added to the
language.

- `tools/fft N [left|right]`, `tools/ifft N`: sine window both ways; the
  frame, the packing and the scale are in tools/fft.h.
- `--solo a,b` and `--mute a,b` on the engine's command line: from the
  first sample, the same part gains `solo` and `mute` set. A name that
  isn't a play stops the engine.
- `tools/add [--delay M] FILE ...`: streams summed in step.
- patches/spectrum_freeze.aither: an example.

The delay is N / 2 samples, named on the command line, whatever
AITHER_BUFFER is (no machine in patches). One instrument back beside the
band:

    tools/add --delay 512 <(aither song.aither --mute horn) \
      <(aither song.aither --solo horn | tools/fft 1024 | ... | tools/ifft 1024) | tools/play

## Measured

- fft then ifft: the signal N / 2 late, within 5e-8 (float32), at frame
  sizes 4 to 4096, whole frames or not. tests/pipe_tools.c.
- Through a live engine in the middle: the same, 1.5e-8, frames aligned,
  no late periods.
- `--solo` of each play adds back to the patch within 3e-8; `--mute low`
  and `--solo high` are identical on a two-play patch. The add line above
  is the patch delayed by 512, within 3e-8.
- A brick wall at 1 kHz written as a patch (`input() * (if bin < 22 then
  1 else 0)`): 220 Hz 0.3001 → 0.2998, 3 kHz 0.2 → 0.00007 (69 dB down).
- spectrum_freeze with the input cut at 1.2 s: rms 0.172, 0.174, 0.173 at
  1.5, 2 and 2.5 s; 440 Hz about 100 times 900 Hz's level, where the
  input had nothing.
- Live, uConsole, tesla_jam's horn through the chain beside the rest of
  the band, three engines at once, 60 s (notes/multicore/pacer.c): 17
  late periods, all in the first 15 s on TCC; none after -O2 landed.

## What it doesn't do

- **A solo is the part as the mix has it.** The final line still runs,
  so its reverb and buses come too, and what the final line makes that
  isn't a play can't be soloed. A part that reads a muted part hears it
  silent (a kick's sidechain goes). What's nonlinear in the final line
  won't add back exactly.
- **Muted plays still compute,** so running a patch twice costs twice the
  CPU. Parking what nothing heard needs is the speed-up, not done.
- **Feedback through the chain** needs a fifo, and the loop's delay then
  includes the pipes and the engine's block: the one place the machine
  would be heard. The same question as far coupling; open.
- **Stereo is two chains.** One side, or their mean, goes through.
- **A patch between the tools must start with the stream,** on the
  command line: sent to a running engine, its frames wouldn't line up.
- **Long convolution** doesn't fit a stream of bins; it would be a tool
  of its own, `tools/convolve room.wav`. Not built.
