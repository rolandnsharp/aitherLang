# Real-time audio: one core, real-time priority

**Status:** built 2026-10-19 (realtime.c). Follows the latency work of
2026-10-18 (see the journal for that day).

## Why

The engine computes a block, then waits on the pipe; `play` waits on the
card. If the kernel is late to wake either, the card runs dry. Measured
on the uConsole with a cyclictest-style tester (one thread woken every
200 µs, five minutes, the other cores loaded flat out with `spectra`):

| | worst wake-up |
|---|---|
| normal priority, any core (2026-10-18) | 18 ms |
| real-time priority, its own core (2026-10-19, an A53 on battery) | 1.6 ms (average 19 µs; 4 of 1.5 million over 1 ms) |

A block is 11.6 ms at 512 frames, 2.9 ms at 128, so a reserved core at
real-time priority is enough. A real-time kernel would mean porting the
uConsole's patches to 6.12 or later, and isn't needed.

## What it does

`realtime.c`, called once by the engine's mix thread (live only; offline
renders don't), `tools/play` and `tools/card` (after it starts its
command, so the command chooses its own):

- **The audio core:** the highest-numbered core online, the last A72 on
  mains, the last A53 on battery when the A72s are off. `AITHER_RT_CPU`
  overrides.
- **`SCHED_FIFO` 70** (`AITHER_RT_PRIO`), with reset-on-fork so a child
  starts at normal priority. The kernel keeps 5% of every second for
  everything else, so a runaway patch can't freeze the machine.
- **Memory locked**, only when `RLIMIT_MEMLOCK` is unlimited: with a small
  limit, locking future allocations would make later ones fail.
- **Cores come and go** (the A72s switch off on battery): the kernel moves
  a pinned thread off a core that goes offline, so the engine's doctor
  pins the mix thread again about once a second to the current audio
  core. `play` and `card` are light, and fall back to any core. Checked
  2026-10-19 by taking core 5 offline and back with an engine playing:
  the mix thread went 5, 4, 5.
- Without permission it says so once and runs as before. `AITHER_RT=0`
  turns it off.

The background `-O2` compiler, the server and the doctor stay at normal
priority, off the audio core's real-time slot. A command still gets the
lock first (lockForCommand): the mix yields before its next block.

## Permission

`~/uconsole/install/aither-realtime.sh` (with sudo) installs
`/etc/security/limits.d/90-aither-realtime.conf`: `rtprio 80` and
`memlock unlimited` for roland. Log out and in; `ulimit -r -l` then says
80 and unlimited.
