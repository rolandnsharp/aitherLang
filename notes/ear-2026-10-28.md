# aither as an ear

**Status: saved to come back to (2026-10-28, Roland, with Claude).**
Nothing built. So far aither has been a voice, making signals; its
primitives (pairs as complex numbers, `spin`, `analytic`, `freq_shift`,
`dho` returning its phasor, `$state` integrators, stepped arrays, any
rate, `input()` on the pipe) are as much those of hearing, radio and
measurement. These are the directions where signals come in.

## 1. A cochlea (first)

The ear is a bank of damped oscillators: `dho` and `resonator`. The mic
through `input()` into a few dozen resonators spaced as the ear spaces
them (a log or ERB scale, 50 Hz to 8 kHz), each one's envelope drawn as
a moving cochleagram in `tv` and heard as the bank rings.

- About 30 lines of aither; no language change expected. Writing it
  will show what is missing (a cleaner envelope follower, a bank as a
  def over a table of frequencies).
- It is the front end of Zara's oscillator-first recogniser (an ear of
  oscillators on the uConsole) and item 2 of the field plan
  ([field-plan.md](field-plan.md), "an ear for Zara").
- The cochlea as an instrument is not an experiment. Once it feeds a
  recogniser that is trained or evaluated, it is model work: register it
  on experiments.blah.dev before the first run (~/.claude/CLAUDE.md).

## 2. Measuring, on `card`

`tools/card` (built 2026-10-17, [card-2026-10-16.md](card-2026-10-16.md))
gives a fixed, known loop delay between the sound card's output and
input. What it was built for are patches not yet written:

- a lock-in amplifier: send a tone, multiply what comes back by `spin`
  at the same frequency, low-pass: the amplitude and phase of the
  response, far below the noise;
- a swept sine: a room's, a speaker's or a filter's response;
- the metal detector and cable tracer from
  [sensing-2026-10-16.md](sensing-2026-10-16.md);
- passive listening: natural radio and lightning (VLF) through the
  card's input.

## 3. Radio

The output is already IQ-shaped and runs at any rate
(`AITHER_RATE=192000 aither fm.aither | sdr-tx`). Receiving is
`input()` from an SDR, `freq_shift` to tune, `analytic` and `phase` to
demodulate: FM, AM and single sideband as a few lines each. The gap is
rates: an SDR gives 2.4 MHz and the ear wants 48 kHz, and a voice runs
at one rate. Decimation has no primitive yet (stepped arrays' `every`
hints at the shape); leave it until radio is being received.
